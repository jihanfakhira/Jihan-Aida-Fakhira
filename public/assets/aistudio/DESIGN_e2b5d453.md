## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a tactile, archival stationery aesthetic engineered as a digital physical portfolio desk. It evokes feelings of intimate craft, meticulous curation, and warm nostalgia. The visual language merges archival manila folders, loose-leaf punched sheets, polaroid prints, washi tape, and rubber stamps with high-end editorial typography.

The target audience consists of design leaders, creative directors, and discerning collaborators seeking intentionality, personality, and tactile craftsmanship in a digital landscape often dominated by homogenized flat design.

### Design Movements & Visual Grammar
- **Tactile Skeuomorphism & Trompe L'œil:** Physical paper cutouts, realistic drops, paperclips, binder punch holes, and physical rotation angles.
- **Editorial Archival:** Strict metadata indexing, monospace filing labels, rubber-stamped verification marks, and loose-leaf grid lines.
- **Craft Contrast:** Deliberate juxtaposition of clean modern typography against delicate handwriting and expressive script signatures.

## Colors

The palette simulates physical paper stocks, archival card folders, and stamping inks resting on an untreated desk surface. There is no traditional dark mode; the aesthetic relies exclusively on the tactile warmth of archival papers.

### Color Rules & Roles
- **Surface Canvas (`desk-cream`, `#EFECE1`):** The primary desk plane. Forms the backdrop for all floating stationery components.
- **Primary Ink (`ink-charcoal`, `#1A1A1A`):** Pure typewriter charcoal. Used for primary typography, borders, and structural outlines.
- **Folder Tint (`folder-pink`, `#E6A6B8`):** Dusty pastel rose manila stock used for folder jackets and structural file containers.
- **Secondary Stationery Papers (`card-blue`, `card-sage`, `card-burgundy`, `paper-form`): Dedicated paper stocks representing specific document types:
  - `card-blue` (`#C4D7ED`): Periwinkle correspondence letters, bio dossiers, and project cards.
  - `paper-form` (`#F4F1EA`): Ruled loose-leaf notes and tabular binder sheets.
  - `card-sage` (`#9BA892`) & `card-burgundy` (`#9E4763`): Index cards and brochure panels.
- **Interactive Stamp (`stamp-blue`, `#2345B6`):** Official rubber stamp impression tone. Used for system verification stamps, badges, and high-priority interactive accents.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances four distinct roles:
1. **Display Script (`Pinyon Script`):** Expressive signatures, personal headings, and decorative vector strokes. Never used for functional UI labels.
2. **Editorial Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Structural headings, card titles, and body letters. Upper-case styles use extended letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to emulate vintage print titling.
3. **Monospace Docket (`Space Mono`):** File indexes, archive stamps, catalog numbers, and formal form field labels. Always uppercase for labels.
4. **Handwritten Notes (`Caveat`):** Organic user notes, informal margin annotations, and simulated hand-filled binder fields.

### Viewport Adjustments
Body copy must not shrink below 13px on mobile viewports. Expressive script headers use proportional scaling, falling back to clean display sizes below 768px.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model simulates layered stationery arranged on a desktop surface. Elements rely on controlled overlaps, rotational skews, and staggered multi-column grids rather than rigid, edge-to-edge containers.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (≥1024px):** Asymmetric 12-column freeform canvas with intentional layer offsets. Sections maintain vertical breathing space of `80px` to `110px`. Overlapping cards create depth through stacked z-indices and tilt physics.
- **Tablet (769px–1023px):** 6-column modular grid with reduced overlaps and unified rotation bounds.
- **Mobile (≤768px):** Single-column stacked dossier layout. Section margins compress to `1.25rem`, with element rotations constrained to `±2deg` to prevent horizontal overflow.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation is achieved through physical paper physics rather than digital volumetric shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy & Tiers
- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Global background desk (`#EFECE1`) covered with a subtle fractal SVG paper grain (`feTurbulence`, `opacity: 0.04`, `mix-blend-mode: multiply`).
- **Layer 1 (Under-peeking Elements):** Rotated loose sheets, cards, and file tabs tucked beneath primary cards. Drop shadow: `0 10px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 2 (Primary Dossiers & Folders):** Punched binder sheets, bio letters, and project dossiers. Drop shadow: `0 18px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.14)`.
- **Layer 3 (Foreground Overlays):** Polaroids, index clips, and sticky notes resting on top of sheets. Drop shadow: `0 14px 28px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.16)`.
- **Layer 4 (Fasteners & Metal):** Metallic silver stars, washi tape swatches, and paperclips that mechanically bind layers together.
- **Layer 5 (Sticky App Bar):** Fixed top nav with translucent fill (`rgba(239, 236, 225, 0.92)`), `backdrop-filter: blur(6px)`, and hairline bottom ink border.

### Tilt Physics
Elements use random or staggered rotational tilts between `-6deg` and `+6deg` on desktop to simulate natural desk scatter. On card hover, elements transition to `translateY(-6px) rotate(0deg) scale(1.02)` via easing `--ease: cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`.

## Shapes

The design system uses crisp, right-angle geometry (`0px`) for paper sheets, business cards, polaroids, and dossier envelopes to preserve authentic paper guillotine cuts. 

Selected exceptions apply where real-world stationery dictates specific curves:
- **Manila Tabs:** Rounded top corners (`12px 12px 0 0`) matching die-cut file folders.
- **Punched Binder Rings:** Three circular negative-space cutouts (`14px` diameter) along the left margin of loose-leaf cards.
- **Interactive Pills & Badges:** Full-round pill silhouettes (`9999px`) used for rubber stamps, status tags, and tactile button links.
- **Polaroid Frames:** Sharp outer edges with asymmetric padding (`12px 12px 38px`) creating the classic wide bottom caption lip.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Pill CTA:** Off-white (`#FFFFFF`) or charcoal (`#1A1A1A`) fill, fully rounded border (`9999px`), `1.5px solid #1A1A1A`, uppercase `Space Mono` typography. Tactile offset shadow: `2px 2px 0px #1A1A1A`. Hover shifts the shadow to `4px 4px 0px #1A1A1A` with a `-2px` translation.
- **Text Action Links:** Uppercase sans-serif with an animated bottom hairline underline that expands outward on hover.

### Manila Folders & Tab Dossiers
- Die-cut top tab (`220px` width, `28px` height) with `12px 12px 0 0` rounded corners matching the folder body (`#E6A6B8`).
- Left edge features three circular margin punch-holes revealing the desk surface below.
- Thin horizontal and vertical divider rules in `rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.18)` segment inner document sections.

### Cards & Stationery Sheets
- **Loose-Leaf Dossier Sheet (`paper-form`):** Flat rectangular sheet with faint ruled lines, red or grey left margin line, and monospace form field metadata.
- **Periwinkle Bio Letter (`card-blue`):** Clean editorial layout with two-column sans body copy, stamped signature, and rotated tilt.
- **Polaroid Photo Frame:** Solid white perimeter border (`10px` sides and top, `38px` bottom), containing photo with subtle grayscale or vintage tint. Accented with an overlapping silver paperclip SVG.

### Badges, Chips & Rubber Stamps
- **Rubber Stamp:** Rotated (`-4deg`) container with `1.5px dashed var(--stamp-blue)` border, stamped text in uppercase `Space Mono` or `Pinyon Script`, and high-contrast blue ink (`#2345B6`).
- **Archive Skill Tags:** Pill-shaped capsules filled with card pastel tones (`card-sage`, `card-burgundy`, `card-blue`) with uppercase monospace labels.

### Input Fields & Binder Forms
- **Field Wrappers:** Modeled as archival forms. Labels are uppercase `8.5px Space Mono` set above a bottom-bordered writing line (`1px solid rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.22)`).
- **Value Displays:** User input or default values render in `Caveat` script (`21px`), mimicking handwritten pen ink filling out a printed document.

### Fasteners & Decorative Assets
- **Paperclips & Washi Tape:** Translucent washi tape strips (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)`) positioned across card edges; metallic paperclips binding polaroids to underlying papers.
- **3D Silver Stars:** Chrome metallic star SVGs placed sparingly across section transitions to add depth and whimsical craft contrast.
